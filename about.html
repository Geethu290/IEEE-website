🌐 About IEEE

The Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) is the world's largest technical professional organization, dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity. Founded in 1963, IEEE publishes some of the most highly cited technical journals in electrical engineering, electronics, and computer science.

IEEE's mission is to foster technological innovation and excellence for the benefit of humanity. Through its global network of members, publications, and conferences, IEEE connects engineers and technologists worldwide.

🏫 IEEE SB LBSITW

The IEEE Student Branch at LBS Institute of Technology for Women, Thiruvananthapuram, empowers students through technical workshops, networking events, and professional development opportunities.

Our branch connects aspiring engineers with global IEEE resources and fosters a collaborative learning environment where students can grow beyond the classroom. We organize regular seminars, hands-on labs, and industry interaction sessions throughout the academic year.

⚡ About IEEE Ignite

IEEE Ignite is a two-day multi-track technical festival crafted to spark curiosity, reward innovation, and build collaborative skills. The event brings together students from disciplines ranging from computer science to electrical engineering under one roof.

Ignite features three distinct tracks — Technical, Professional, and Interactive — ensuring every participant finds their spark, whether they are a seasoned coder, a budding entrepreneur, or a curious newcomer looking to level up their career game.

  • Foster technical skill-building through hands-on workshops in web development, AI/ML, and competitive programming.
  • Bridge the gap between academia and industry through career guidance talks and professional development sessions.
  • Cultivate teamwork and creative problem-solving through interactive challenges and innovation pitches.
  • Create a vibrant community of student innovators connected through the global IEEE network.
  • Recognize and celebrate student talent through prizes, certificates, and mentorship opportunities.
